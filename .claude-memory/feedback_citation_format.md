description: User requires journal-volume-page-year citation format in all documents, not author-journal-year format
type: feedback
---

In-text citations must use the format: *Journal* **Volume**, Pages (Year)
Example: *ACS Catal.* **14**, 2696–2708 (2024)

NOT: Li et al. (ACS Catal. 2024) or Li et al. 2024

**Why:** User explicitly corrected this format. They want precise bibliographic identification inline, not vague author-year references.

**How to apply:** In all research plans, literature reviews, and technical documents, use the journal-volume-page-year format for in-text citations. The full author list should only appear in the References section at the end.
